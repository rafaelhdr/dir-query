This project helps you ask questions about your own documents. Upload PDFs and, in a future version, ask questions and get answers grounded in what you uploaded.

What is RAG?

RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It's a way of answering questions by first retrieving the most relevant pieces of your own documents, then giving those pieces to a language model so it can generate an answer grounded in your content, instead of relying only on what the model already knows.

This is a beta version. Right now you can upload PDF documents, but they are not yet processed, indexed, or searchable — the "Ask" page does not answer questions yet.
