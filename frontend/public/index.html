This project helps you ask questions about your own documents. Upload PDFs to a workspace, then ask questions and get answers grounded in what you uploaded.

What is RAG?

RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. It's a way of answering questions by first retrieving the most relevant pieces of your own documents, then giving those pieces to a language model so it can generate an answer grounded in your content, instead of relying only on what the model already knows.

Workspaces

A workspace keeps a group of documents separate from any other — for example, one per person or company. Uploading and asking questions both happen inside a workspace, so different workspaces never mix their documents. Browse or create a workspace to get started.
